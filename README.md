# SignalGuru — monthly allocation

The pages:

- **https://signalguru.github.io/report/** — this month's allocation
- **https://signalguru.github.io/report/history.html** — the long-run record since 1996

A record of one proprietary tactical allocation model's own monthly positioning and results.
Updated once per month, a few days after the prior month closes.

This repository contains the published pages and nothing else. The model, its rules and its
research are not here and are not public.

**Not investment advice.** Nothing on that page is a recommendation, an offer or a
solicitation, and none of it is personalised advice. Figures shown are the model's own returns,
gross of trading costs, spreads, slippage and taxes. Past results do not predict future returns.
